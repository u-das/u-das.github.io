🌱 About Me

Hi, I’m Urmi Das — a science enthusiast, genetics researcher, and proud cat companion 🐾. I’m endlessly fascinated by the hidden patterns of life — from the tiniest twists in our DNA 🧬 to the big-picture questions that shape who we are.

By day, I dive into the world of genomics and molecular biology, exploring how our DNA shapes health, behavior, and disease. I love turning complex data into meaningful insights that can improve lives.

By night (and weekends!), you’ll find me surrounded by indoor plants, tending to my little jungle 🌿, or out in the garden with my hands in the soil 🌸. I find peace in nature and joy in nurturing things—whether it’s a seedling, a research idea, or a curious mind.

This site is a little window into my world—where science meets soul, and curiosity never stops growing. Thanks for visiting! ✨
